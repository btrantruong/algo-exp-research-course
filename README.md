## Course Description

This practical seminar focuses on the impact of algorithmic curation, with a particular focus on social media algorithms. Over two semesters, students will gradually build causal experiments to investigate the societal or psychological consequences of these algorithms. Possible studies span from how algorithmic design choices influence which content becomes visible or popular, to how different levels of user control affect user behavior or public attitudes. In the first semester, the course introduces relevant concepts behind the experiment framework and algorithms, while students simultaneously develop their research questions, experimental designs, and study materials. In the second semester, they will conduct the experiment, analyze the data, interpret, and present their findings.
